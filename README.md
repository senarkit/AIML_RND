3. Workbook-3: Linear Layer [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/Gs9OTWsd9NM)]  
`This video walks through a set of skillfully designed exercises to explore the linear layer, showing how it transforms input data by multiplying inputs with weights and adding biases – a simple yet powerful operation in neural networks. The workbook, along with the video demonstrates how linear layers aid in predictions and pattern recognition, making it essential for understanding deep learning.`
4. Workbook-4: Activation [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/4TdwtP3qgIQ)]  
`This video of the workbook covers activation functions which introduce non-linearity to neural networks. It explains how they determine whether a neuron should be activated based on its inputs, enabling networks to capture complex patterns and relationships in data. The video discusses the basics of common activation functions like ReLU, Sigmoid, and Tanh, explaining their roles in deep learning and how they impact a network’s performance, making neural networks powerful and effective.`
5. Workbook-5: Artificial Neuron [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/3mAGf-e5Qa8)]   
`The video of this workbook explores the concept of an artificial neuron, the basic unit of a neural network that mimics the behavior of a biological neuron. It depicts how they process inputs, apply weights, calculate a weighted sum, and use activation functions to generate outputs. It also concentrates on the role of these artificial neurons in forming the building blocks of deep learning models.`
6. Workbook-6: Batch [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/tv4uUq_jD3w)]   
`In this video, the concept of batches in deep learning is explained, along with how processing data in smaller groups improves efficiency. It explores why batches are important to balance memory usage and computational power. Furthermore, the distinctions between various types of batches are examined, with appropriate examples provided to illustrate their advantages and disadvantages. This video also details how to optimize a particular model while speeding up and stabilizing neural network training.`
7. Workbook-7: Connection [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/-CIIaivcZ_Q)]  
`The video of this workbook provides a clear concept of connections, focusing on how neurons are linked across layers to form a network. It illustrates how weights define these connections and influence information flow and learning through the model. Moreover, it covers their role in feedforward and fully connected networks and their impact on performance and complexity. The video demonstrates how connections enable neural networks to process inputs and generate accurate predictions.`
8. Workbook-8: Hidden [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/BRyY_v52Cn4)]  
`In this video, the basics of hidden layers is discussed, highlighting their importance as the intermediate layers between input and output. The exercises exhibit how they process and transform data through weighted connections and activation functions, enabling the network to capture complex patterns and relationships. The video further analyzes the advantages and limitations of adding more hidden layers within a network.`
9. Workbook-9: Deep [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/OwGoBOCIIi8)]  
`The video of the workbook covers the definition and importance of ‘deep’ in deep learning, emphasizing the role of having multiple layers in a neural network. It clarifies how deeper networks, with several hidden layers, allow models to capture more complex features and patterns in data. It also illustrates the challenges and benefits of increasing the depth of a network, including the trade-offs in performance, computation, and training time.`
