6. Workbook-6: Batch [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/tv4uUq_jD3w)]
`In this video, the concept of batches in deep learning is explained, along with how processing data in smaller groups improves efficiency. It explores why batches are important to balance memory usage and computational power. Furthermore, the distinctions between various types of batches are examined, with appropriate examples provided to illustrate their advantages and disadvantages. This video also details how to optimize a particular model while speeding up and stabilizing neural network training.`
7. Workbook-7: Connection [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/-CIIaivcZ_Q)]
`The video of this workbook provides a clear concept of connections, focusing on how neurons are linked across layers to form a network. It illustrates how weights define these connections and influence information flow and learning through the model. Moreover, it covers their role in feedforward and fully connected networks and their impact on performance and complexity. The video demonstrates how connections enable neural networks to process inputs and generate accurate predictions.`
8. Workbook-8: Hidden [[Tutorial](https://youtu.be/BRyY_v52Cn4)]
`In this video, the basics of hidden layers is discussed, highlighting their importance as the intermediate layers between input and output. The exercises exhibit how they process and transform data through weighted connections and activation functions, enabling the network to capture complex patterns and relationships. The video further analyzes the advantages and limitations of adding more hidden layers within a network.`
